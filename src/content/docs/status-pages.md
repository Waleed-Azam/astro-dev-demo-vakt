Every Vakt workspace includes a hosted status page at `yourco.vaktstatus.com` — or `status.yourco.com` via CNAME.

## What's included

- **90-day uptime bars** per component, generated from real check data (see [our own](/status))
- **Incident timeline** with draft → investigating → resolved workflow
- **Subscriber notifications** — email + webhook, with per-component subscriptions
- **Custom branding** — logo, colors, custom domain and password protection on Pro

## Incident workflow

Open incidents manually or let monitors open them automatically. Templates keep comms consistent: acknowledge in one click, post updates from Slack with `/vakt update`, and publish a postmortem that links the timeline automatically.
